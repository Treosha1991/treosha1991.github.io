Use terms

Effective 10 November 2026

Viral Physics Publisher is an owner-operated personal utility for preparing and uploading original animations to the owner's connected YouTube channels. This website is informational. It does not provide a public upload service, subscriptions or paid accounts.

Authorization and content

Use the utility only with channels you own or are authorized to manage, and with video, image, text and audio content you have the right to upload. The channel owner controls the upload queue, visibility and publication time. The utility does not acquire ownership of uploaded content.

YouTube services

The application uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube remains subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and applicable YouTube policies. API availability, processing and quotas are controlled by Google and YouTube.

Operation of the desktop utility

The queue worker runs on the owner's computer. The owner is responsible for reviewing queued content and keeping the computer and authorized connection available. An upload may require attention after a connection, processing or authorization error. The owner can stop the worker or revoke the application's Google access at any time.

Privacy

The privacy policy describes the information used by the application, local storage and access controls. This website does not accept videos or Google credentials.
